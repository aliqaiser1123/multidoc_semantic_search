Human history spans from early hominid evolution to modern interconnected global societies. It began with agricultural revolutions that fostered the first civilizations in river valleys, such as Mesopotamia. Over millennia, powerful empires rose and fell, spreading culture, religion, and trade. The Middle Ages saw the expansion of global networks and the Renaissance sparked cultural rebirth. Subsequently, the Enlightenment and Industrial Revolution dramatically transformed science, technology, and governance. Today, the world is characterized by rapid technological advancement, global integration, and complex international relations, all shaped by the enduring legacy of countless past events.
